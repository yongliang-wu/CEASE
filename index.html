Overview

CEASE removes concepts from diffusion models as requests arrive over time. It suppresses interference between successive edits to preserve unrelated generations and the effects of earlier erasures.

Method

CEASE uses two subspace constraints in a solver that requires no training. It keeps the shared replacement representation invariant and, when interference is detected, projects the current update away from dominant output directions of accumulated past updates.

Results

100 sequential edits

Evaluations cover continual erasure of 100 celebrities, artistic styles, or instances on Stable Diffusion v1.4, v1.5, and v2.1.

CEASE achieves the most consistent balance between erasure and preservation among the evaluated methods. It maintains recognizable retained concepts and coherent unrelated generations as edits accumulate.
